Where your visit came from

If you arrive from one of our ads, the page address carries a short campaign code, the search phrase the ad matched and Google's ad click identifier. None of them contains your name or contact details. If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, the search phrase and click identifier are not kept or passed on.

When you click through to the seller

Our paid link takes you to the seller's website by way of ClickBank. ClickBank and the seller set their own cookies and, if you buy, process your order under their own privacy policies, not ours. They report sales back to us with the product, the amount and the codes above. We never receive your name, address or card details.
