Serbian chef knife vs a finger-hole chef knife

A Serbian chef knife is a tall, thick, heavy blade shaped somewhere between a chef knife and a cleaver, usually with a hammered, hand-forged look. A finger-hole chef knife is an ordinary chef-knife shape with a hole for your index finger. Both often have a hole in the blade; they're built for different jobs.

01Side by side

What to expect
ShapeSerbian: very tall, almost rectangular blade with a curved belly. Finger-hole chef: regular chef-knife profile, tapering to a point.
WeightSerbian: heavy; the weight does much of the chopping. Finger-hole chef: lighter, closer to an everyday kitchen knife.
The holeSerbian: sellers describe it as for hanging, for a finger, or both. Finger-hole chef: made for the index finger, as a grip.
Good atSerbian: chopping through big vegetables, boneless meat, herbs in bulk, outdoor and grill cooking. Finger-hole chef: everyday slicing, dicing and mincing.
Less good atSerbian: fine, precise cuts and tip work; it tires the wrist. Finger-hole chef: heavy chopping; it's not a cleaver.
SteelMany Serbian knives sold online are carbon or high-carbon steel that can rust if left wet. The finger-hole knife we cover is listed as stainless.

02Which one, for whom

Serbian, if

  • you cook outdoors or on a grill a lot
  • you break down big squash, cabbages, roasts
  • you like a heavy knife doing the work

Finger-hole chef, if

  • you want one knife for daily cooking
  • your grip slides or tires
  • you'd rather not oil carbon steel after every use

03Things to check on any Serbian knife listing

  1. Steel type. Carbon steel takes a great edge but needs drying and a little oil. If it doesn't say, ask.
  2. Weight in grams or ounces. Over about 400 g (14 oz) is a lot for daily use.
  3. "Hand-forged" in the photos only. A hammered pattern can be stamped. If hand forging matters to you, look for who made it and where.
  4. Return window. Heavy knives are personal. Make sure you can send it back.

··Quick answers

What is a Serbian chef knife used for?

Heavy chopping: big vegetables, boneless meat, herbs in bulk, and a lot of grill and camp cooking. Its height and weight do much of the work.

Why does a Serbian chef knife have a hole?

Sellers describe it as a place to hang the knife, a place for a finger, or both. It isn't the same thing as a chef knife designed around a finger hole.

Is a Serbian knife a cleaver?

Close, but not quite. It has a curved belly for rocking cuts and is usually thinner than a bone cleaver. It isn't meant for chopping through bone.
