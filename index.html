06Quick answers

Does a finger hole actually make a knife steadier?

It puts your index finger where a trained cook's pinch grip would be, so the blade is less likely to twist or roll. If you already pinch-grip, you won't gain much. It doesn't make the edge any sharper.

Can left-handed cooks use it?

The hole goes right through the blade, so in principle either hand fits. We couldn't confirm how the handle shape feels in a left hand, so treat that as unknown and keep the order e-mail for the 60-day guarantee.

Is this the same as a Serbian chef knife?

No. A Serbian chef knife is a tall, heavy, cleaver-like blade. This one is a regular chef-knife shape with a hole for your finger. How the two compare.

How do I clean the hole?

By hand, with a soapy sponge pushed through it, then dry it right away. Skip the dishwasher. Full care routine.
